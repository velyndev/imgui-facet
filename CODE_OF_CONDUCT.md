# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on software. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests
and reviews is expected to:

- argue about the code, the design and the documentation, not about the people;
- be patient with questions, including basic ones;
- give reasons when declining a change, and accept that a change may be declined;
- keep harassment, insults, sexual content and other people's private information out of it.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block accounts that do not keep to
this.

To report a problem, use GitHub's report link on the comment or the profile, or reach the
maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile.
